This website

The site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or tracking scripts. It's hosted by Cloudflare, which handles your IP address and browser details to deliver pages and protect the site from attacks. We don't keep logs of visits.

Veer

Veer plans a bike route and sends it to your Garmin device. To do that it uses the following.

Your location and destination

When you plan a route, the app sends your current location as the start, the destination and any stops you add to our route server. Veer only asks for your location while the app is open, and only to plan from where you are. It doesn't track you in the background.

Route files and shared links

If you open a GPX, TCX or FIT file in Veer, the app sends the route in it to our server to preview it and convert it for your device. If you share a Google Maps or Apple Maps link into Veer, our server follows that link to find the place. Google or Apple see a request from our server, not from you.

How long we keep routes

Planned and uploaded routes are stored for up to a day, so your Garmin device can download them, and are then deleted automatically. They aren't linked to a name, email or account.

Your IP address

Our server uses the IP address of each request to limit how many requests one address can make, which stops abuse. It isn't stored or linked to your routes.

Services the app uses

  • Route planning: our server sends the start, stops and destination to BRouter to work out the route. BRouter receives the coordinates, but not your IP address.
  • Place search: the text you type in the search box is sent from your phone to Photon, a search service run by komoot. Photon sees your IP address and the search.
  • Maps: map images come from OpenFreeMap. Your phone downloads the area you're looking at, so OpenFreeMap sees your IP address and that area.
  • Your Garmin device: routes reach your device through Garmin's Connect app, under Garmin's privacy policy.
  • Payments: purchases go through the App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment details. Apple and Google send us sales totals, not who bought.

Who processes data for us

Cloudflare hosts this website and the route server, and stores routes for the day they're kept. The services above handle data as described there. Some of these providers process data outside the UK, including in the EU and the US.

Why we use it

We use this data to provide what you ask the app to do, which is planning and sending routes, and to keep the service secure. In UK GDPR terms, those are performing our contract with you and our legitimate interest in protecting the service.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete personal data we hold about you, or object to how we use it. Because routes are deleted within a day and there are no accounts, we usually hold nothing that identifies you. Email {{email.privacy}} and we'll reply within a month.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.
