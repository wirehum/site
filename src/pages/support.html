Veer

Veer sends a route from your phone to a Garmin Edge mid-ride, without stopping the activity.

A route didn't arrive

  • Check the Veer data field is on a data page of the ride profile you're using. It can be on any page, including the map, and doesn't need to be showing. Garmin only runs it while it's in the active profile.
  • Check the Garmin Connect app is running on your phone and shows the Edge as connected.
  • The Send button shows how the last send went. If it reports an error, include that wording when you email.

Starting the route on the Edge

Go to Navigation, then Courses. The route is always called Veer. The Edge's own navigation takes over from there, with turn prompts and rerouting.

Your data

Veer has no accounts. Routes are kept on our server for up to a day so your Edge can download them, then deleted automatically. The privacy policy has the details.
