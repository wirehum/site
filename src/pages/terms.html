Riding safely with Veer

Veer plans routes automatically from open map data, which can be incomplete, out of date or wrong. A route may include closed roads, busy roads, unsuitable surfaces or paths cycling isn't allowed on.

  • Check a route before you ride it, and use your own judgement on the road.
  • Follow the law, road signs and conditions over any directions from Veer or your device.
  • Don't use your phone while you're riding. Stop somewhere safe first.
